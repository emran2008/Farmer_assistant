🐟 Pond Culture

Pond culture is a traditional and popular method of fish farming where fish are cultivated in managed ponds.

  • Suitable pond size selection
  • Water quality management
  • Proper fish stocking density
  • Regular feeding
  • Disease monitoring

💧 Biofloc System

Biofloc technology uses beneficial microorganisms to improve water quality and reduce water exchange.

  • High stocking capacity
  • Water quality monitoring
  • Carbon source management
  • Proper aeration
  • Regular feeding control

♻️ RAS

Recirculating Aquaculture System continuously filters and reuses water for fish farming.

  • Mechanical filtration
  • Biological filtration
  • Water circulation
  • Oxygen management
  • Temperature monitoring

🎣 Cage Culture

Cage culture is a fish farming technique where fish are raised inside floating or fixed cages in natural water bodies.

  • Suitable cage location
  • Proper cage size
  • Stocking management
  • Regular feeding
  • Net and cage maintenance

🌊 Marine Culture

Marine aquaculture involves cultivating aquatic organisms in seawater environments.

  • Water salinity monitoring
  • Suitable marine species
  • Feed management
  • Water quality monitoring
  • Environmental management
